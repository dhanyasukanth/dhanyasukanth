about

Not just another cloud jockey.

I’m Dhanya Sukanth B.K., a Cloud Administrator based in Bengaluru with over two years of experience in cloud infrastructure, application security and production support.

At Microland I work with global enterprise clients. Today I manage the Akamai, Imperva Cloud WAF and SecureSphere platforms for Baker Hughes: onboarding and decommissioning applications, managing SSL/TLS certificates, and troubleshooting DNS and traffic issues across cloud and on-premises environments.

Before that I supported GE’s MyApps global infrastructure, covering AWS operations, Linux server maintenance and patching, L3 support, and a monitoring stack built on Grafana, Prometheus, Alert Manager and OpenSearch.

I hold a B.E. in Electronics & Communication from VTU and certifications in AWS, Azure and Terraform. I enjoy work where reliability and security meet, and I’m open to new opportunities, including international roles.
